**Sizzle & Serve** — run the busiest street-food stall in town. Tickets land on the order board with a patience timer; you are the chef. Grab buns, patties, lettuce, tomatoes and cheese from the pantry, chop the salad on the board (hold SPACE), grill the patties until they are cooked — not burnt — build each burger to its ticket on a plate, and ring the serve bell before the customer walks away. Four burgers combine the same pantry in different ways (Classic Burger, Cheese Melt, Garden Stack, Double Trouble), the lunch rush ramps up with more tickets and shorter patience, and a 3-minute day ends with your score, coins and best combo.

Played with keyboard and mouse in a desktop browser: WASD or arrows to walk, click to walk somewhere, E (or a click) to use a station, hold SPACE to chop, T to trash, P to pause, R to restart, M to mute. All art is drawn in canvas and all sound is synthesised with WebAudio — the game loads nothing over the network and runs entirely from the static files under `game/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or just open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser.
